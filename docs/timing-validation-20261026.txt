Bounded CubeCell GPS timing, UART and MeshCore checks — 26 October 2026

One physical HTCC-AB02S / Air530 node was tested using the shared repeater-clock
source. The public builder's explicit-identity profile images are separately
compiled; they were not flashed. No private identity, firmware image, precise
position, raw capture or device flash dump is included in this repository.

Observed defect and patch

An advert-signing stress test with the original 255-byte receive buffer took
about 0.97 seconds and discarded 237 GNSS bytes. After enlargement to 1,023
usable bytes, three signing tests took 970.6–971.3 ms, peaked at 678 buffered
bytes and discarded zero bytes. Prepared adverts were released without TX.
Signing remains a blocking main-loop operation.

Twenty requested journal summaries plus the boot checkpoint completed in the
revised bench build. Maximum complete checkpoint duration was 648 RTC ticks
(19.775 ms). Hardware UART overruns, software drops, UART error flags, flagged
flash-delayed PPS edges and anchor errors all remained zero. One NMEA checksum
failure appeared at startup before the signing stress and did not increase.

The RF peer transmitted eight addressed raw MeshCore direct ACK frames: six
unique and two duplicates. The node received all eight, forwarded six and
suppressed both duplicates. The independent peer received each correctly
rewritten forwarded frame exactly once. There were no reported radio CRC,
header, overflow, malformed, recovery, TX-failure or packet-pool errors. All
four packet buffers were free at the end. Peer airtime was 488 ms total.

This RF trial used 125 kHz because the SX1302 peer cannot receive 62.5 kHz.
The actual routing and duplicate handling ran through the pinned MeshCore core.
It does not prove production-PHY range or reception performance. The bench's
airtime estimate remained conservative at the production bandwidth.

Bench PPS observations

Of 73 distinct period status samples, 71 were 1,000,013–1,000,018 us, with a
median of 1,000,015 us. Two acquisition samples were 999,014 and 966,012 us.
Sampled high times were 99,998–100,004 us. Receiver fix quality weakened during
the bench; the PPS counter stayed unchanged across a 49.37-second span of host
status samples, with shorter pauses too. Pending writes deferred, forwarding
continued and writes completed after PPS returned. The electrical outage
duration and cause of reception changes were not independently measured.

Production configuration

The normal 62.5 kHz relay was installed after the bench. A 302.2-second saved
monitoring span contained 145 distinct sampled PPS periods, all between
1,000,012 and 1,000,017 us, median 1,000,015 us, with no sampled outliers or PPS
counter pauses after acquisition. Widths were 99,999–100,004 us. Last status:
eight satellites, HDOP 0.99, GNSS time, 294 counted rising edges and 3,562 valid
NMEA sentences. The one startup checksum failure did not increase. UART loss,
UART error, flagged late-edge and anchor-error counters remained zero.

The roughly +15 ppm period offset is relative to the uncalibrated MCU RTC.
These are software ISR-entry times sampled through status, not every pulse,
hardware edge capture or calibrated absolute UTC accuracy. No wired PPS path
into the host's USB modem inputs was demonstrated.

The bootloader verified the installed image checksum. Installed build, radio,
storage, identity, EEPROM and sticky scheduling claims passed verification.
EEPROM was byte-identical. All journal rows decoded correctly; intentional
circular-log appends replaced oldest rows after a complete local backup.

Validation limits

The normal production build uses the same changed firmware sources as the
bench, with its bench flag absent. The public builder retains its own private
identity generation and compile-time role configuration. Host sanitizers and
all three role tests pass. Public profile builds must pass flash/checksum/RAM
guards; see VALIDATION.json for their current margins.

The finite radio workload used isolated direct ACKs, including arrivals while
signing. It did not test signed-advert saturation, dense bursts, forced recovery,
over-the-air queue overload, originated hourly text over RF, production-bandwidth
RF reception, overnight uptime, surveyed position accuracy or temperature
behavior. A larger UART buffer does not make cryptography nonblocking or
guarantee lossless mesh traffic. Missing PPS still prevents optional writes and
originations; the patch does not create a missing GNSS pulse or improve antennas.
